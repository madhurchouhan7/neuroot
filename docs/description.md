# 1. What is Neuroot?

Neuroot is an AI-powered student operating system/Productivity Ecosystem designed to help students manage academics, productivity, focus, emotional wellbeing, and daily life in one cozy ecosystem.

Neuroot is NOT just another timetable or to-do app.

Neuroot acts like:
- a smart semester planner,
- an attendance tracker,
- an AI study assistant,
- a focus companion,
- a motivational study buddy,
- and a calm emotional support system.

The core philosophy of Neuroot is:

> "Help students survive and grow through college life without burnout."

Neuroot combines:
- academic productivity,
- emotional wellness,
- AI planning,
- cozy gamification,
- and adaptive smart UI

into one intelligent mobile experience.
